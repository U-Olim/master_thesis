\section{Numerical-Domain Diagnostics}
\label{app:numerical-domain-diagnostics}

The primary comparison domain is $A_0=[-1,3]$. Wider domains $A_1=[-3,5]$ and $A_2=[-5,7]$ assess sensitivity to finite search endpoints. Table~\ref{tab:appendix-grid-measures} retains the key frozen domain-measure comparisons; Figure~\ref{fig:grid-domain} summarizes the expansion pattern.

At strong relevance, the accepted set localizes more clearly and expansion adds little accepted measure. Under weak relevance, acceptance can continue toward expanded boundaries. This motivates reporting the fixed-domain accepted-set measure rather than unrestricted confidence-region length. Persistence across wider finite domains indicates poor localization over the investigated ranges, not proof of global unboundedness or failed identification.
