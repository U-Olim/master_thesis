\section{Coverage Forensic Audit}
\label{app:coverage-forensic-audit}

Coverage is evaluated directly at the exact true structural effect $\alpha_0(\tau)$, independently of whether that value lies on the finite candidate grid. The forensic audit checked 60,000 direct truth evaluations: 500 replications for each of 120 sample-size, quantile, instrument-strength, and estimator cells.

Wilson 95\% Monte Carlo intervals exclude nominal $0.95$ coverage in 54 of the 120 cells. The audit finds no missing or nonfinite true-parameter $W$ statistics. The large coverage distortions reported in the main text are therefore neither plotting-grid artifacts nor consequences of missing-value handling. The frozen audit report and supporting diagnostics remain preserved in the repository.
